# Third-party components

## Decimen Optical Transfer

Long-text QR flashing uses the wire protocol and fountain codec from [decimen-optical-transfer](https://github.com/bashalarmistalt/decimen-optical-transfer) (AGPL-3.0-or-later).

The browser decoder loads `decimen-codec` WebAssembly from the same repository via jsDelivr at runtime.
